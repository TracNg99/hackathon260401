New MFRS Standard: Are You Ready for the Change?

Source: accaglobal.com/my (ACCA Malaysia) · masb.org.my · 1 April 2025

This Week's Key Update

ACCA Malaysia, in conjunction with the Malaysian Accounting Standards Board (MASB), has announced a significant update to the Malaysian Financial Reporting Standards (MFRS) framework effective Q3 2025.

The changes affect how businesses recognise and measure financial instruments, revenue recognition under MFRS 15, and lease accounting under MFRS 16. Companies with complex revenue structures or significant lease portfolios should act immediately to assess impact.

ACCA Malaysia is hosting a series of CPD webinars throughout April and May for practitioners needing to upskill on the new requirements.

3 Key Takeaways

1

MFRS 15 revenue recognition rules tightened

Multi-element arrangements must now be unbundled at contract inception. Review your revenue streams.

2

MFRS 16 lease modifications have new treatment

Lease renewals and modifications now require immediate remeasurement of the right-of-use asset.

3

CPD deadline: practitioners must log 40 hours by June

ACCA Malaysia has set a CPD completion deadline. Edge8 can help structure your CPD plan.
