2025 Audit Report: Key Findings & Action Items

Source: audit.gov.my (Jabatan Audit Negara) · 1 April 2025

This Week's Key Update

The National Audit Department of Malaysia (Jabatan Audit Negara) has released its latest compliance findings affecting both public sector bodies and private entities with government contracts.

The 2025 audit cycle highlights recurring weaknesses in procurement documentation, internal control frameworks, and financial reporting timelines across audited entities.

Private sector businesses engaged in government tenders or GLCs are advised to review their audit readiness frameworks and ensure internal audit committees are aligned with the updated standards.

3 Key Takeaways

1

Procurement documentation gaps remain top finding

Entities must ensure complete procurement trails from RFQ through to PO and delivery confirmation.

2

Internal audit committees must be independent

The report flags conflicts of interest in audit committee compositions across multiple entities.

3

Financial reporting timelines tightened

Entities must submit quarterly management accounts within 30 days of quarter-end under revised guidelines.
