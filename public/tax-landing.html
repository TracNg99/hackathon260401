New SSM Filing Requirements: What Every Director Must Know

Source: ssm.com.my · 1 April 2025

This Week's Key Update

The Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) has issued updated guidelines for company directors regarding annual filing requirements effective from April 2025. The changes affect all Sdn Bhd and Bhd registered entities.

Key changes include revised timelines for submission of audited financial statements, new digital verification requirements for director signatures, and updated penalties for late filings under the Companies Act 2016.

Businesses are advised to review their internal compliance calendars and ensure their appointed company secretary is aware of the updated requirements before the Q2 deadline.

3 Key Takeaways

1

New submission deadlines apply from April 2025

Directors must ensure audited accounts are lodged within the revised timeline under updated SSM guidelines.

2

Digital signature verification now mandatory

Physical signatures on annual returns are no longer sufficient. MyCorpID digital verification is required.

3

Penalties increased for late filings

Late lodgement penalties under Section 68 have been revised. Review your compliance calendar immediately.
